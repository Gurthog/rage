# Rage comic archive

The archive helps people discover and browse rage comics.

## Language

**Comic**:
A rage comic presented as an item in the archive. Reposts of the same comic are not different comics.

**Title**:
A brief, human-readable name used to identify a comic. It may be supplied by a maintainer rather than the comic's creator.

**Character**:
A named figure represented in rage comics, such as Trollface or Forever Alone.

**Creator credit**:
An attribution to a known creator of a comic, not a speculative claim from a repost. Most comics may have no known creator.

**Found-at link**:
A link to the place where the maintainer found the archive's current copy of a comic. It does not necessarily identify the comic's origin.

**Tag**:
A curated label for a comic's themes or situations, used to help people discover it. Character assignments are tracked separately.
