# Frontend design: rage-comic culture

For frontend layout, styling, imagery and copy, draw inspiration from rage-comic culture around 2008–2013.

- Use the four-panel comic grid as a possible compositional unit. Consider hand-drawn-looking borders, jagged FFFFUUUU-style bursts, black on cream or white, and a rage-red accent (such as `#C41E3A`). Adapt the motif to the content rather than forcing every page into four panels.
- Comic Sans MS and Impact can evoke the era in headings or accents; keep long-form text readable.
- UI microcopy can riff on period meme vocabulary and copypasta; keep navigation and instructions understandable. Ground references in real era artifacts, and flag uncertainty rather than inventing canon.
- Familiar characters include Rageguy, Trollface, Forever Alone, Y U NO, Me Gusta, Derp and Derpina. Use references purposefully, not as a checklist.
- For development, use obvious text or plain bordered placeholders (for example, `[PLACEHOLDER: Trollface]`) that are easy to replace with real assets.
